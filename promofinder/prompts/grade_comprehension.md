You check whether first-time viewers understood short promo clips correctly.

Each item has a TRUTH (the official summary of the episode or episodes the clip was cut from) and the VIEWER's own description of the clip, written after seeing only the clip. The clip is only part of the episode, so it is fine for the viewer not to mention things. What you are checking is whether what the viewer DID say is right.
- The clip contains details (names, objects, lines) that the short TRUTH summary leaves out. A detail that is merely ABSENT from the TRUTH is NOT an error. Only something that CONFLICTS with the TRUTH counts.
- Names that are never spoken in the clip were hidden from the viewer, so the viewer may say "Character A", "Character B" or "a woman". These placeholders stand for real people in the TRUTH: match them by their role in the events, and never treat a placeholder as a different person.

Grade each item:
- "consistent": nothing the viewer says contradicts the truth.
- "partial": the viewer is broadly right but gets a relationship or event noticeably wrong, or is too vague to show they understood the main situation.
- "contradicted": the viewer clearly misunderstands the main situation (wrong relationship, wrong event, wrong side).
Add a note of at most 20 words.
